O studiuAbout the studio

To ni samo moje delo. Tukaj sem doma.This is not just my job. This is where I am at home.

Sem Sindy Korbelj, nohtna stilistka. Nohte ustvarjam že skoraj osem let in želje, motivacije ali ljubezni do tega dela mi še nikoli ni zmanjkalo. To je moj mir, moja ustvarjalnost in tista iskrica, ki me spremlja vsa ta leta. I am Sindy Korbelj, a nail stylist. I have been creating nails for almost eight years and I have never run out of drive, motivation or love for this work. It is my calm, my creativity and the spark that has stayed with me all these years.

Lepotni studio Shine Bright je nastal 4. januarja 2023, iz sanj, poguma in velike želje. Danes vas sprejmem na Slovenski ulici 42 v Murski Soboti, v prostoru lepote, zaupanja, dobrega počutja in pozitivne energije. Shine Bright beauty studio was born on 4 January 2023, out of dreams, courage and a great wish. Today I welcome you at Slovenska ulica 42 in Murska Sobota, in a space of beauty, trust, wellbeing and positive energy.

Vsako leto me nauči nekaj novega. Znanje še naprej nadgrajujem in sledim novostim, da vam lahko ponudim le najboljše. Every year teaches me something new. I keep building on my knowledge and following what is new, so that I can offer you only the best.

TečajCourse

Combi manikura in 2‑stopenjsko permanentno lakiranjeCombi manicure and two-step permanent polish

Delo z nohti zame ni samo poklic, ampak resnično uživanje, ustvarjanje in nenehno učenje. Zato vrata odpiram tudi vam: vsem, ki bi se rade naučile pravilnih osnov ali nadgradile svoje znanje. Working with nails is not just a profession for me: it is real joy, creating and never-ending learning. So I am opening my doors to you too, to everyone who would like to learn the right basics or build on what they know.

Učimo se, vadimo, delamo napake in rastemo skupaj. We learn, we practise, we make mistakes and we grow together.

Ambasadorka Jana NailsJana Nails ambassador

Ljubezen na prvi pogledLove at first sight

Stranke me pogosto vprašate, kako to, da imam v salonu vse od ene znamke. Odgovor je preprost: ker je to Jana Nails in ker sem se v to znamko zaljubila. Pri njih najdem vse, kar potrebujem za izdelavo nohtov, in z izdelki sem izjemno zadovoljna. Clients often ask me why everything in my salon comes from one brand. The answer is simple: because it is Jana Nails and because I fell in love with it. I find everything I need for creating nails there, and I am extremely happy with the products.

Od maja 2026 sem ambasadorka znamke Jana Nails Slovenija, Shine Bright pa je uradni partnerski salon Jana Nails. Since May 2026 I have been a brand ambassador of Jana Nails Slovenija, and Shine Bright is an official Jana Nails partner salon.
