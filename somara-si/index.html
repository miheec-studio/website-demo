AboutO Somari

Music that grows in the momentGlasba, ki raste v trenutku

Somara is a Slovenian songwriter, singer and artist. Most of her music is born through live looping: voice, breath and instruments layered in the moment, often improvised and unplanned.

Many of the sounds come straight from the forest: her steps, the rustle of leaves, the touch of trees. Her songs draw on Slavic folk tradition and mythology, from ritual calls for Vesna and Morana to the forest spirit Leshy, and on mantras she carried home from Nepal.

Somara je slovenska kantavtorica, pevka in umetnica. Večina njene glasbe nastane z live loopingom: glas, dih in inštrumenti se nalagajo v trenutku, pogosto improvizirano in neplanirano.

Veliko zvokov prihaja naravnost iz gozda: njeni koraki, šelestenje listja, dotik dreves. Njene pesmi črpajo iz slovanskega ljudskega izročila in mitologije, od obrednih klicev Vesni in Morani do gozdnega duha Leshyja, ter iz manter, ki jih je prinesla domov iz Nepala.

“Tides” was never planned. It appeared the moment I stopped trying to create something intentional and simply played what was already there.

»Tides« ni bila nikoli načrtovana. Pojavila se je v trenutku, ko sem nehala poskušati ustvariti nekaj namerno in sem preprosto zaigrala to, kar je že bilo tam.

~ Somara ~
