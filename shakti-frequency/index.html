O meniAbout me

Pot ni vedno lahka. Postane pa lažja, ko se nehamo upirati. The journey isn’t always easy. It becomes much lighter when we stop resisting.

Sem Nataša Percel, facilitatorka ženskega pristopa k prebujanju Kundalini Shakti energije. Delam ob podpori nežne Seichim energije, z zvokom in z zavedanjem, kako sta v telesu povezana um in srce.

I’m Nataša Percel, a facilitator of a feminine approach to awakening Kundalini Shakti energy. I work with the support of gentle Seichim energy, with sound, and with an awareness of how mind and heart are connected in the body.

Zadnjih pet let tudi sama hodim skozi intenziven proces notranje transformacije. Kundalini je moja zaveznica: na površje prinaša vse moje ranjene dele, da jih lahko pogledam, spoznam in vzljubim. Skozi ta proces rastem, se lupim in se vedno znova odkrivam.

For the past five years I have been going through an intense process of inner transformation myself. Kundalini is my ally: it brings all my wounded parts to the surface so that I can face them, get to know them and learn to love them. Through this process I grow, I shed and I rediscover myself, again and again.

Pri delu sledim ritmu tvojega živčnega sistema, spoštujem tvoj edinstven prostor tolerance in z razumevanjem travm držim varen okvir. Nikoli ne boš prisiljen/a v proces.

In my work I follow the rhythm of your nervous system, respect your own window of tolerance and hold a safe, trauma-aware space. You will never be pushed into the process.

Kako poteka seansaThe session

Vse, kar pride, je dobrodošlo Whatever arises is welcome

  1. Udobno se namestišYou settle in

    Na joga blazino, preprogo ali posteljo. Prostor naj bo prijeten in varen, da se lahko sprostiš.

    On a yoga mat, a rug or your bed. Let the space feel pleasant and safe, so you can relax.

  2. Prepustiš se glasbiThe music carries you

    Dlani obrneš navzgor, zapreš oči in se prepustiš glasbi, ki te povabi na nežno, a mogočno zvočno potovanje.

    You turn your palms upwards, close your eyes and surrender to music that invites you on a gentle yet powerful journey of sound.

  3. Uglasim se s tebojI attune to you

    Vzpostavim prostor za notranjo povezavo in se uskladim z izvorno, materinsko frekvenco ter z edinstveno energijsko vibracijo tvojega bitja.

    I open a space for inner connection and align with the original, motherly frequency and with the unique energetic vibration of your being.

  4. Energija stečeThe energy flows

    Z namero odprem pretok Seichim energije in jo usmerim v tvoja energijska središča, da se raztopi zastala energija in gostota v tvojem avričnem polju. Vodim jo intuitivno, tja, kjer te lahko najbolj podpre.

    With intention I open the flow of Seichim energy and guide it into your energy centres, so that stagnant energy and density in your auric field can dissolve. I guide it intuitively, to wherever it can support you most.

  5. Telo se izraziYour body speaks

    Morda bo tvoje telo spontano zaživelo: z gibi, petjem, jokom, tresenjem, smehom ali plesom. Morda boš začutil/a le tišino in mir. Ni napačnega načina, obstaja le tvoja pristnost.

    Your body may come alive on its own: with movement, singing, tears, trembling, laughter or dance. Or you may feel only silence and peace. There is no wrong way, only your authenticity.

  6. Nežna vrnitevA gentle return

    Ko se glasba zaključi, te nežno prebudim iz procesa, ponudim prostor za deljenje in ti predam usmeritve za integracijo.

    When the music ends, I gently bring you back, offer space for sharing and give you guidance for integration.

Energija je skrivnostni dih življenja Energy is the mysterious breath of life

Kundalini Shakti

Nevidna, neotipljiva, neizmerljiva. Kot nežna, svetlobna reka neprestano teče skozi vsako celico našega bitja. Kundalini Shakti energija počiva na dnu hrbtenice. Ko se prebudi, na svoji poti raztaplja bolečinska jedra in prebija blokade v vseh naših telesih, da lahko energija lahkotno teče.

Invisible, intangible, immeasurable. Like a gentle river of light it flows ceaselessly through every cell of our being. Kundalini Shakti energy rests at the base of the spine. When it awakens, it dissolves knots of pain on its way and breaks through blockages in all our bodies, so that energy can flow with ease.

Naša življenjska sila oslabi, ko v sebi nosimo neozdravljene rane: travme, karmične sledi, zaščitne oklepe in maske, ki so nas nekoč varovale, danes pa nas le še omejujejo.

Our life force weakens when we carry unhealed wounds: traumas, karmic imprints, protective armour and masks that once kept us safe but today only hold us back.

Seichim

Seichim ima korenine v starodavnem egipčanskem sistemu zdravljenja. Je energija, ki je globoko usmerjena v srce in nas vodi k ljubezni, božanskemu ženskemu principu in višjemu namenu. Pogosto jo doživljamo kot nežno, mehko in razširjajočo.

Seichim has its roots in an ancient Egyptian healing system. It is an energy deeply centred in the heart, guiding us towards love, the divine feminine principle and a higher purpose. It is often felt as gentle, soft and expansive.

To je sveta frekvenca, s katero so v templjih delovale svečenice pod vodstvom Sekhmet in Isis, boginj zdravljenja in preobrazbe.

It is the sacred frequency the priestesses worked with in the temples, guided by Sekhmet and Isis, goddesses of healing and transformation.

Zvočna masaža, moja meditacija Sound massage, my meditation

I place singing bowls on and around the body and sound them gently with a soft mallet. Their sound and vibration travel through the body and attune it, while you simply let go.

Delo z zvokom sem poglobila na izobraževanju v Studiu Sistrum (februar 2026). Poleg skled uporabljam tudi druga naravna zvočila.

I deepened my work with sound in a training at Studio Sistrum (February 2026). Besides the bowls I also use other natural instruments.
